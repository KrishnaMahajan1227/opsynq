# Phase 23 Role Matrix

## Company Owner / Company Admin
Full company workspace and administration.

## Operations Manager
Action Center, execution packages, agencies, beneficiary execution/imports, logistics visibility, technician material, installed assets/service, SLA, agency performance, compliance, approvals, analytics, notifications, documents, automation and readiness.

## Program Manager
Programs, Contracts/LOA, Work Orders, Work Packages, Agencies, Beneficiaries, Imports, Claims, SLA, Agency Performance, Compliance, Analytics, Notifications, Documents, Audit and Readiness.

## Inventory Manager
Inventory Overview, Item Master, Warehouses, Stock, Scanner, logistics/shipments visibility, Technician Material, Reconciliation, Analytics, Notifications, Documents and Readiness.

## Procurement Manager
Item Master, Warehouses, Procurement, Stock, Analytics, Notifications and Documents.

## Finance User
Claims & Receivables, Analytics, Notifications, Documents and Audit.

## Quality User
Action Center, Beneficiary Records, Installed Assets, Asset Lifecycle, Service/Warranty, Warranty & AMC, Reconciliation, SLA, Agency Performance, Quality & Compliance, Analytics, Notifications, Documents, Audit and Readiness.

## Logistics Manager
Warehouses, Stock, Scanner, Logistics Overview, Shipments & Tracking, Drivers & Vehicles, Technician Material, Reconciliation, Analytics, Notifications, Documents and Readiness.

## Viewer
My Workspace, Executive Overview, Analytics, Notifications and Documents only.

No unrelated module is exposed as a "view-only" module. Navigation search, Ctrl/Cmd+K, My Workspace and global-search destinations all use the same role-filtered module registry.
