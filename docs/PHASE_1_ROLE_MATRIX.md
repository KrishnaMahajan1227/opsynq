# Phase 1 Role Matrix

## Platform
### platform_superadmin
- Platform dashboard
- View/search all company tenants
- Approve, suspend, reactivate or archive companies
- Inspect company details and company users
- Create tenant-bound company users
- Access platform-level summary metrics

No additional Platform roles are introduced in Phase 1 by design.

## Company roles scaffolded
- company_owner
- company_admin
- operations_manager
- program_manager
- inventory_manager
- procurement_manager
- finance_user
- quality_user
- logistics_manager
- viewer

Company workspace permissions will be activated module-by-module as those modules are implemented. Backend role checks must remain authoritative.

## Agency
Existing Agency roles and business workflows remain unchanged in this phase.
