# Phase 44 — AI Operations visibility & Supply Chain control tower

## What changed
- AI Operations is a first-class Company module and also has a compact global shortcut in the workspace toolbar.
- AI Operations now separates pending proposals from decision history so users can see what automated monitoring prepared and what people approved/rejected.
- Every automated proposal remains human-gated. A confirmation dialog is required before the approval request is submitted.
- Supply Chain Control was simplified into a clear operating-flow card, four daily workspaces, focused metrics, exceptions, capacity and recent dispatch activity.
- Contextual AI Operations buttons were added to procurement, stock, fleet, service and finance surfaces where assisted monitoring is useful.
- Provider/model branding remains absent from product UI.

## Control principle
Detect → Prepare → Review → Manual Confirm → Execute → Audit.

No controlled purchase, dispatch, payment, destructive delete or regulated status transition is finalized solely by automation.
